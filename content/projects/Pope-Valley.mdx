Pope Valley is an upcoming ultra-luxury, low-density residential oasis designed for those who appreciate the finer things in life. Inspired by the serene landscapes of Napa Valley but deeply rooted in the heart of the national capital, this project seamlessly blends architectural elegance with lush, sweeping green ecosystems.
💎 Configuration & Pricing

3 BHK Luxury Suites

Size: 2,800 sq. ft.

Price: ₹4.50 Cr onwards

4 BHK Grand Residences

Size: 4,200 sq. ft.

Price: ₹6.80 Cr onwards

💡 Key Highlight: Every apartment features expansive 10-foot-deep running balconies looking out over beautifully manicured internal valleys and water features, ensuring a completely private, estate-like feel.

🌟 State-of-the-Art Amenities

Pope Valley is built around a central, sunken curated garden called The Valley, which hosts premium lifestyle amenities:

The Vatican Club: A massive 35,000 sq. ft. clubhouse featuring an indoor heated pool, a temperature-controlled lap pool, and a luxury spa/sauna resort.

Fitness & Leisure: Fully equipped cross-fit gym, private mini-theater (15-seater), and a sky-lounge bar on the 18th floor.

Sports Zone: Sunken tennis courts, squash courts, and a professional-grade cricket simulator.

Eco-Features: IGBC Gold Certified green building, EV charging stations at every parking bay, and advanced 5-tier biometric AI security.
